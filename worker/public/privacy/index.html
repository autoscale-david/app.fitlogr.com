Privacy

fitlogr is free fitness tracking. We ask only for the access required to estimate meals and show your day.

  • Your Google account is your fitlogr identity.
  • Meal photos stay on your phone. fitlogr does not keep a permanent copy of photo files.
  • A photo that looks like food is sent to our API and to OpenRouter so a model can estimate food and nutrition. Photos are not used to train models.
  • Weight, workouts, steps, heart rate, and calorie burn are read from Apple Health or Android Health Connect only after you allow it, then stored so your history is available on your devices.
  • We do not sell personal health information and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can export your fitlogr records and delete your account. Deleting your account does not delete photos in your phone library.

Questions: support@fitlogr.com
